## Selected Work

### AI Systems & Engineering Automation

At ShristyVerse, I work on AI systems for understanding engineering drawings and converting them into structured representations and parametric CAD.

My work includes:

- Multi-stage AI orchestration
- Structured intermediate representations
- Engineering information extraction
- Review and verification workflows
- Backend execution services
- Execution tracing and artifact persistence
- Benchmark-driven evaluation

The system improved reconstruction success from 60.6% to 69.7% and reduced empty-OCR failures from 36.4% to 15.2% on a 68-drawing benchmark.

I am particularly interested in this class of systems because it combines machine learning with deterministic engineering logic: models handle ambiguity and perception while structured computational pipelines enforce consistency and correctness.

### NagarAI — Civic Infrastructure Intelligence

NagarAI is an AI-powered civic infrastructure platform designed to convert visual observations of urban problems into structured, actionable information.

The system combines multimodal vision-language models with a backend and geospatial processing layer to identify and classify civic issues such as potholes, waterlogging, open manholes, and encroachment.

Technologies include:

`Qwen2.5-VL` `Gemini` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Next.js` `TypeScript`

The platform generates structured information including issue type, severity, affected area, and bilingual Hindi/English descriptions, followed by geographic visualization and reporting.

[View the NagarAI repository](https://github.com/Akaksingh/smartstreetcv)
